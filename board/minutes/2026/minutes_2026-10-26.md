### Ask To Leave
- Rechner has proposed updating our bylaws to implement a system similar to Noisebridge's Ask To Leave process.
- Currently, board intervention is required to remove a problematic person from the space. We would like to empower people to handle these issues without being required to involve the board, both for independence and for response time.
- As discussed during the last meeting, this change may be best implemented when we update the bylaws for the motions and voting rework.
